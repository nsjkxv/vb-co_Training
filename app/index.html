Frequently Asked   Questions

What documents do I need to work legally in Poland?

To get legal employment in Poland, you’ll typically need a valid work permit, a visa (if you're from outside the EU), and a signed contract with the employer. Don’t worry — VB&CO helps you handle all of this step by step.

How long does the recruitment and visa process take?

It usually takes between 2 to 6 weeks, depending on the job and where you're applying from. We’ll guide you through the process and keep you updated along the way.

Do I need to speak Polish to get a job?

Not always. Many roles, especially in IT, logistics, or manufacturing, don’t require Polish. English is often enough — and we’ll let you know upfront what’s needed for each job.

Can I apply for jobs from outside of Poland?

Yes! Most of our candidates start their journey from abroad. We specialize in employment abroad and offer full support for relocation and visa paperwork.

Do you charge job seekers for your services?

No — our services are completely free for job seekers. We’re paid by the companies that hire you.

What types of jobs do you offer?

We work with employers across different industries — from IT jobs in Poland and skilled trades to warehouse, logistics, and healthcare positions. Both remote and on-site roles are available.
